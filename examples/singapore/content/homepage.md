SG10K Health provides population-scale genomic variant data from Singapore's
diverse population, enabling precision medicine research across Chinese, Malay,
and Indian ancestries.

### Dataset

- Multi-ethnic Singaporean cohort
- Chinese, Malay, and Indian ancestry groups
- Processed with gnomAD QC pipeline

### Example Genes

- [PCSK9](/gene/PCSK9) — Proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9
- [BRCA1](/gene/BRCA1) — Breast cancer type 1 susceptibility protein

### About

Part of the **National Precision Medicine** programme (npm.sg).
